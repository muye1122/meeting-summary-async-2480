【详细方案】
Understanding: The buyer needs a handover interface for membership managers with three states (pending_claim, returned_for_info, claimed) and strict role scoping. A director sees the whole department; a membership manager sees the department claim queue plus handovers they claimed or returned; a signing consultant sees only the handovers they signed. The package may be edited only by the signing consultant and only while the handover is pending_claim or returned_for_info; after each edit the missing-field list is recomputed and a returned item with no missing fields automatically returns to pending_claim. A claim is rejected with 422 HANDOVER_INCOMPLETE while fields are missing, must be exclusive under concurrency (one winner, 409 for the rest) and must respect a director's designated manager (403 for anyone else). A return requires a reason of at least 10 characters, sets returned_for_info and notifies the signing consultant. After a successful claim, a transactional outbox delivers an idempotent CRM upsert keyed by contract_id covering enterprise, contact, manager assignment, planned onboarding date and contract number, retries at 1/5/30/30 minutes and, after five failures, marks the sync failed and notifies the technical lead. The CRM is not ready, so a local stub is provided and the real integration point is documented in the README.
