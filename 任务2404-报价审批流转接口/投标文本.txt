【详细方案】
Understanding: The buyer needs an approval flow in which the quote tier decides how many people must sign off and in what order. The approval record must pin the exact calculation that was submitted by storing its SHA-256, so any later change to the calc_snapshot blocks further decisions. Step records must be append-only: an approval writes a pending row per step, and each action appends a new immutable row rather than updating the old one, which keeps a complete audit trail. The current step is the latest pending row. Access is strict: only the approver assigned to that step can approve or reject; rejects carry a mandatory comment of at least 10 characters; withdraw belongs to the submitter and only while pending. Time-based escalation must be testable without waiting real days, so the clock is injected and the reminder sweep is deterministic and idempotent. Finally, an approved quote carries a 15-day validity window and publishes a quote.approved event.

1. Model sales_quote_approvals and sales_quote_approval_steps as in-memory tables and ship the matching SQL migrations with append-only guidance.

Acceptance criteria - every customer criterion addressed explicitly:
- Criterion 1 (a non-current-step approver calling approve returns 403): a site_manager quote is submitted with a supervisor as current approver; the later club manager and an unrelated actor both receive 403 NOT_CURRENT_APPROVER, and the step log stays unchanged.
- Criterion 2 (tampered snapshot returns 409 QUOTE_CHANGED_SINCE_SUBMIT): after submit, a test helper changes calc_snapshot; approve and reject both compare the recomputed SHA-256 against the stored hash and return 409 with expected and actual digests.
- Criterion 3 (site_manager needs supervisor then club manager): the routing service produces two steps; the supervisor's approval only advances current_step to 2, and only the club manager's approval sets the approval and quote to approved with valid_until at injected time plus 15 days and a quote.approved event.
- Criterion 4 (reject comment under 10 characters returns 422): 9-character and blank comments return 422 COMMENT_TOO_SHORT with the measured length, while a 10-character comment succeeds and sets the quote to rejected.
- Criterion 5 (48h pending notifies the club manager under a frozen clock): the clock is frozen, a site_manager quote is submitted, and the sweep is run at 23h, 25h and 49h; only the approver is reminded at 25h, the manager is notified at 49h, and a repeat sweep produces no duplicates.
